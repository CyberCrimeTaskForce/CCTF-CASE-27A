### 1. CASE EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

On October 1, 2026, at approximately 02:47:00 UTC, Dr. Elena Rostova—Chief Cryptographic Architect and Head Security Specialist at Nexus Dynamics Inc.—disappeared under high-threat indicators from her secured facility office located in Building B, Room 304.

First-responding corporate personnel and initial law enforcement units discovered Room 304 unsecured and unoccupied. Her primary forensic workstation was recovered in an active execution state, running a live countdown process transmitting network packets toward an external, public decentralized repository network identified as "project Aether Archive". Concurrently, an automated data-purge protocol was initiated across internal corporate volumes.

You have been designated as Lead Investigators with the Cyber Crime Task Force. Your mandate is to conduct a forensic reconstruction of the digital trail left in the wake of the incident, determine whether Subject Rostova was forcibly extracted or executed a strategic whistleblower evasion, and intervene before critical evidence is permanently destroyed.

---

### 2. THE CORE INVESTIGATIVE QUESTION

All preliminary evidence and physical findings must be evaluated against two competing investigative theories:

* **Theory Alpha (Corporate Espionage / Extraterritorial Abduction):**  
  Subject Rostova was targeted, incapacitated, and forcibly extracted by hostile corporate competitors seeking root cryptographic signing keys and proprietary infrastructure backdoors.

* **Theory Beta (Strategic Whistleblower Evasion):**  
  Subject Rostova uncovered evidence indicating Nexus Dynamics leadership repurposed internal neural network architecture—designated **SENTINEL-AI**—into an unconstitutional mass-surveillance and domestic tracking mechanism. Anticipating administrative retaliation or physical incapacitation, Subject staged an emergency evacuation while deploying an off-grid evidentiary breadcrumb trail across distributed version control networks.
